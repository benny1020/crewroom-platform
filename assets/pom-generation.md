# Pomeranian plush character assets

Date: 2026-10-21. Mode: built-in image_gen, two reference-guided generations. Pet photographs guide the dog's identity; the existing plush atlas guides material and studio style. Original photographs and their metadata are excluded from public source. Final PNGs are retained outside the repository and exported to WebP quality 90 with lossless alpha. No platform model call or runtime dependency was added.

Input roles: images 1-3 are identity references of the user's pet; image 4 is STYLE ONLY showing the existing plush teammates. Use the pet's exact face/mane/color identity and the fourth image's premium fleece-plush studio aesthetic. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background and clean alpha edges, no white rectangle, no painted checkerboard, no environment. No humans, human hands, furniture, food, wrappers, lettering, captions, numbers, brands, logos, watermarks, vehicles or frames. Recognizably the SAME real pet shown in images 1-3, transformed into a premium soft fleece-plush character. Preserve the dog's soulful round face and short muzzle; do not turn it into a fox, corgi, teddy bear or generic yellow puppy. ONE character only, front-facing head and short upper body, one cream paw raised in a little greeting, sweet open smile with a tiny pink tongue, full complete mane and ears. A square composition with the whole silhouette in the central 76% of the frame and 12% transparent outer margins. Readable at 48px: distinct honey-gold mane, cream muzzle and dark eyes. No props. Production avatar only.
